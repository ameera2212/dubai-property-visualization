Loading map…
Highest number of sales: Al Barsha South Fourth (community 681), with 3,925 sales.

Is this map useful for identifying the highest-sales community? Not directly. The map uses color to represent median sale price per square meter, rather than sales volume. A community with a high median price does not necessarily have the most transactions. The sales count in the tooltip is more useful for comparing activity.
