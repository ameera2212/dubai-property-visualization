How to Explore

Use the market dropdown to compare off-plan and existing apartment sales. Hover over marks for details. Click any bedroom category in the heatmap to highlight matching sales in the scatter plot and the corresponding comparison bar. Double-click the heatmap to clear the selection. Scroll and drag within the scatter plot to zoom and pan.

Data & Interpretation

Residential flat sales dated January 2023 through August 2026, filtered to 20–500 m² and AED 100,000–20 million. Prices per m² are calculated as transaction value divided by area. Heatmap and bar chart use medians. Heatmap groups with fewer than 10 sales are excluded. These are descriptive associations, not causal explanations.
