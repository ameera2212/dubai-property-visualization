About This Analysis

This dashboard explores residential apartment sales in Dubai from January 2023 through August 2026. It excludes transactions outside the selected size range of 20–500 m² and transaction price range of AED 100,000 to AED 20 million. Median prices describe observed transaction patterns and do not establish the causes of price differences.
